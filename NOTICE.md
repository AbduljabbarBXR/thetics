# Third-party notices

## Exercise media and reference data

Exercise photographs, animated form loops and exercise descriptions are
sourced from [free-exercise-db](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db),
released into the public domain under **The Unlicense**. Images have been
resized for mobile delivery. The animated loops were generated locally from the
project's two reference frames for each movement; they are two-frame form loops,
not continuous video. The Thetics workout programs are original programming
inspired by widely documented training methods; they are not official
reproductions of any coach's paid material.
